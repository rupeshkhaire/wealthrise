Advanced FinOps Cheat Sheet

Quick-fire reference points curated directly from the masterclass files.

Settlements & Infra
  • DvP vs FoP Delivery vs Payment (cash/securities move conditionally) vs Free of Payment (no linked cash).
  • Key CSDs DTC (US Domestic), Euroclear/Clearstream (ICSDs for Eurobonds).
  • SWIFT Settlement (MT54x)
    MT540: Rx FreeMT541: Rx vs Pay MT542: Del FreeMT543: Del vs Pay
Corp Actions Lifecycle
  • Classification Mandatory (No action, e.g. Split), Voluntary (Action required, e.g. Tender), Choice (DRIP).
  • Timeline Record Date (snapshot) → Ex-Date (trades without entitlement) → Pay Date.
  • SWIFT Corporate Actions
    • MT564: Notification
    • MT565: Client Instruction
    • MT567: Status/Ack
    • MT566: Payment Confirm
Derivatives & ISDA
  • Linear vs Non-Linear Linear (Forwards, Swaps, Futures) have symmetrical risk. Non-Linear (Options) have capped downside/unlimited upside.
  • Option Greeks Δ Delta (Price), Γ Gamma (Δ rate), Θ Theta (Time), ν Vega (Vol).
  • Margin (ISDA/CSA) IM (Initial Margin): Upfront to cover potential future exposure. VM (Variation Margin): Daily MTM cash movement.
Fixed Income Mathematics
Pricing Formula
Dirty Price = Clean Price + Accrued Interest
Buyer pays dirty price at settlement. Accrued calculates days since last coupon.
Credit Ratings Floor The line between Investment Grade (IG) and High Yield (HY) sits at: BBB- (S&P/Fitch) / Baa3 (Moody's)
Duration vs Convexity Duration: % price change for 1% yield change (linear estimate). Convexity: Curvature correction (bond prices rise faster than they fall for identical yield shifts).
Reconciliation
  • Three-Way Recon Compares:
    1. Internal Expected Entitlement
    2. Custodian Notification (MT564)
    3. Actual Receipt (MT566)
  • IBOR vs ABOR IBOR: Investment Book of Record (Real-time, includes unsettled). ABOR: Accounting Book (Settled only).
