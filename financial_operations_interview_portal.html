Operations Cheat Sheet

Quick-fire reference points for interviews.

Settlements
  • DvP vs FoP Delivery vs Payment (vending machine) vs Free of Payment (sending a gift).
  • Key Vaults DTC (US), Euroclear/Clearstream (Europe).
  • SWIFT Messages
    MT540: Rx Free MT541: Rx Pay MT542: Del Free MT543: Del Pay
Corp Actions
  • Classification Mandatory (Automatic), Voluntary (You must act), Choice (Happens anyway, pick how).
  • Dates Record Date (VIP List) → Ex-Date (Ticket Cut-off) → Pay Date.
  • SWIFT CA Messages
    • MT564: Notification
    • MT565: Client Instruction
    • MT566: Pay Confirm
Derivatives
  • Linear vs Non-Linear Linear (Forwards, Swaps) are straight bets. Non-Linear (Options) act like insurance.
  • Margin Initial (IM): Security deposit. Variation (VM): Daily settling up.
  • CCP (Middleman) The Central Counterparty steps in the middle of trades to prevent bankruptcy contagion.
Bonds & Reconciliation
Bond Pricing
Dirty Price = Clean Price + Accrued Gas
Buyer pays dirty price. Accrued interest is like paying for the gas left in a used car.
Credit Ratings Line The cliff between Safe (Investment Grade) and Risky (Junk) sits at: BBB- / Baa3
3-Way Recon To find out who made the mistake, Ops checks:
  1. What we calculated (Expected)
  2. What the mega-bank expected (MT564)
  3. What actually landed (MT566)
